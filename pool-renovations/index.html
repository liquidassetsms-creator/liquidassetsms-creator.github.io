What we renovate

Gunite pools are built to last decades, but the finish isn't. When plaster gets rough, stained or starts to flake, when tile pops off the waterline, or when coping cracks, the shell underneath is usually fine. We drain, prep, and refinish so it looks and feels new.

While it's empty

A drained pool is the cheapest time to fix plumbing, add lights, or upgrade equipment. We'll walk the whole system with you before the new finish goes on.

Renovation work

  • Replastering and finishes
  • Waterline tile
  • Coping
  • Plumbing and light repairs while drained
  • Equipment upgrades at the same time
