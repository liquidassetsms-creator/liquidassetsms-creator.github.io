Weekly pool service

Swim on Saturday. We'll handle Tuesday.

A weekly route visit that keeps the water clear and the equipment honest, from a CPO-certified company.

Request a consultation(228) 697-4376

What happens on a visit

Our tech tests the water and balances it, skims the surface, brushes the walls and steps, empties the skimmer and pump baskets, and vacuums when it's needed. Then they look over the equipment: pressure, leaks, noises, error codes.

If something's starting to go, you hear about it from us first, with a photo and a straight answer on whether it can wait.

Why weekly matters on the Coast

Gulf Coast heat, pollen and summer storms swing a pool fast. A pool that's checked every week rarely turns green, and a pump that's watched rarely dies without warning. Catching a weeping seal or a failing capacitor early is the difference between a small repair and a new motor.

Already have a green pool?

We do start-ups and clean-ups too. Tell us what it looks like when you request a consultation and we'll quote the clean-up before weekly service begins.

Homes, apartments and HOAs

We service residential pools and commercial pools alike: apartment communities, HOAs, hotels and condos. Commercial accounts get the same weekly visit plus the water-chemistry records the health department expects to see.

Every visit

  • Water test and chemical balance
  • Skim, brush walls and steps
  • Empty skimmer and pump baskets
  • Vacuum as needed
  • Equipment check: pressure, leaks, codes
  • Heads-up on anything starting to fail
