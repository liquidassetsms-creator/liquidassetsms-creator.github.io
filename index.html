About Us

Liquid Assets MS is a licensed pool builder and service company in Vancleave, Mississippi. We design and build custom gunite pools, renovate the ones that have earned a second life, repair the equipment that runs them, and clean them every week so you never have to think about it. One company, start to finish, from Bay St. Louis to Pascagoula.

Our Story

Scott Warren started Liquid Assets in 2015 as one man and a truck answering pool calls on the Coast. He went on to earn the Certified Watershape Foreman credential through Watershape University, the PHTA Certified Pool & Spa Operator certification, and Mississippi residential contractor license R21335. The company has grown, but it still runs the same way: the owner knows your pool, and you can reach him.

Our Mission

Build pools that are engineered right, not guessed at. Fix what is actually broken and tell you the truth about the rest. And once the pool is in, keep it clear, balanced and running every week of the year. We want to be the last pool company you ever call.

Your Gulf Coast pool builder and service company.

Whether you are dreaming about a brand-new pool and spa, a worn-out gunite pool that needs new plaster and tile, a pump that quit on a Friday, or simply a crew that shows up every week and keeps the water clear, this is what we do, all day, on the Coast.

Every job is run by a licensed, insured, Watershape-trained contractor who answers the phone. No sales desk, no call center, no guessing.

Custom pools, designed and built start to finish

Gunite pools, pool and spa combos, spools, tanning ledges, sheer descents and fire features. We design around your yard and how your family actually swims, then build it ourselves from layout and steel to plaster, equipment and start-up.

New Pools

Renovations that give the old pool a second life

Replastering, waterline tile and coping for gunite pools that are structurally sound but worn out. While the pool is empty we fix plumbing, add lights and upgrade equipment, so it comes back better than new.

Repairs and equipment, diagnosed right and fixed once
