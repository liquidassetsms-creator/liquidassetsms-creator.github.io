About

Family-owned in Vancleave since 2015.

Liquid Assets MS started as one man and a truck answering pool calls on the Coast. It still runs the same way: the owner knows your pool.

Accountability, mostly

We do weekly service, repairs, equipment, renovations and new construction for homes and commercial pools along the Mississippi Gulf Coast. The common thread is that we stand behind the work and tell customers the truth, including when the right answer is the cheaper one.

Credentials

Scott Warren holds the PHTA Certified Pool & Spa Operator certification and is a Certified Watershape Foreman through Watershape University. The company is a member of the Pool & Hot Tub Alliance, holds Mississippi Board of Contractors residential license R21335, and carries general liability insurance.
